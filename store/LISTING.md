## Description (4000 max)

```
Sway Tower is a one-tap stacker with a twist: the tower sways.

A bar slides back and forth. Tap to drop it. Whatever hangs over the edge gets sliced off, and the next bar is that much narrower. Build as high as you can.

THE TWIST
The taller your tower, the more it sways — and the bar you drop doesn't sway with it. A good drop means timing the slide and the sway together. A sloppy drop kicks the tower toward the overhang. A PERFECT drop calms it, and wins a sliver back.

TWO WAYS TO STACK
• Slide: the bar runs across the top of the tower. Tap and it lands.
• Drop: the bar runs one bar above the tower. Tap and it falls onto wherever the top has swayed to.
Same rules, same slices, same three lives. Each mode keeps its own best.

A NEW WORLD EVERY SEASON
Every world is hand-made and has its own song — real instruments for the cartoon worlds, our own synths for the neon ones:
• Sky Islands: a floating island, a countryside far below, up through the clouds into a starry night.
• Toy Kingdom: a block castle, a toy fort with marching guards, and a rocket that blasts off.
• Synthwave and Cyberpunk: neon nights, a chrome car, a rain-slick megacity.
• Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Eve and St. Patrick's Day arrive on their dates — or pick any world any time from the Skins menu.

FEEL IT
Haptics hit when you nail a perfect, hit harder every fifth in a row, and thump when the tower finally goes over and blows up.

MEDALS
Thirty medals for tall towers (5, 10, 15 … 100 bars) and perfect streaks (3, 6, 9 … 30 in a row), in bronze, silver and gold. Game Center leaderboards for Slide and Drop are coming in the next update.

SIMPLE BY DESIGN
No tutorial, no timers, no energy, no upgrades. One tap. Just you and the sway.

• Plays offline — everything ships in the app
• No ads
• No in-app purchases
• No tracking, no account, no data collected
• Works on iPhone and iPad
```

## App Review notes

```
Sway Tower is a single-player arcade game. Everything is in the app: no account, no sign-in, no server, no in-app purchases, no ads, no third-party SDKs besides Capacitor (the web view wrapper). It works with Airplane Mode on.

How to play: tap PLAY, then tap the screen to drop each bar. The bar slides back and forth; whatever overhangs the tower is sliced off. A drop that misses the tower costs a life; three misses end the run. "Drop" (the second card on the title screen) is the same game with the bar released from one bar above the tower.

The Skins button on the title screen lists every world, including the seasonal ones that otherwise appear by date (Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's, St. Patrick's Day), so all content can be reviewed on any date.

Music and sound effects are original, rendered from CC0 recordings; the fonts are open-licensed (SIL OFL / Apache 2.0, listed in the app's font-licenses.txt). All art is our own.

Haptics use the Taptic Engine (UIFeedbackGenerator through Capacitor Haptics); they can't be turned off separately but respect the system setting.
```
