## In one line

Build as high as you can — the tower sways.

## In a paragraph

Sway Tower is a one-tap stacker with a twist: the tower sways. A bar slides back and forth;
tap to drop it, and whatever hangs over the edge gets sliced off. The taller the tower, the
more it sways — and the bar you drop doesn't sway with it — so a good drop means timing the
slide and the sway together. A sloppy drop kicks the tower toward the overhang; a PERFECT drop
calms it and wins a sliver of width back. Two modes (Slide and Drop), three lives, thirty
medals, nine hand-made worlds with their own music, and every run ends with a topple and a bang.

## The game

- **Sway is the hook.** The bar you drop does not move with the tower, so a tall tower is a
  moving target. Sloppy drops kick it harder; a perfect drop calms it and wins a sliver back.
- **Slide or Drop.** Slide lands the bar where you tap. Drop releases it from one bar above, so
  it falls onto wherever the top has swayed to by then. Same slices, same perfects, same three
  lives; each mode keeps its own best.
- **Nine worlds, one for every season.** Sky Islands (the default), Toy Kingdom, Synthwave,
  Cyberpunk, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Eve and St. Patrick's Day. The
  holiday worlds arrive on their dates; any world can be picked any time. Each has its own
  set-piece in the first ten seconds and a story that climbs with the tower.
- **Original music.** One theme (a waltz in six-eight) arranged nine ways: seven rendered from
  CC0 recordings of real instruments (saxophone, French horn, euphonium, piano and drums
  wherever they fit), two on our own synths for the neon worlds. Past 40 bars the song speeds up
  with the slider, time-stretched live so the pitch never changes.
- **Haptics with intent.** The Taptic Engine fires on every perfect, harder every fifth in a
  row, and thumps when the tower finally goes over and blows up. Ordinary drops stay silent.
- **Thirty medals.** Tall towers (5, 10, 15 … 100 bars) and perfect streaks (3, 6, 9 … 30 in a
  row), in bronze, silver and gold.
- **Simple by design.** A title screen with PLAY and two mode cards; while stacking, nothing on
  screen but the scene, the tower, the bar and the score. No tutorial, no timers, no energy, no
  upgrades, no notifications.
